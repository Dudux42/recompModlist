# Gen1 Battle Art Replacer

Replaces front battle art for all 151 Pokémon with selectable Red/Blue, Yellow, Crystal, FireRed/LeafGreen, Emerald, Platinum, HeartGold/SoulSilver, or Gen 5 collections. Supported collections include normal, shiny, static, and animated presentation. Shiny Variation Provider API v1 supports optional per-species alternate shiny front art while Battle Art retains loading, timing, filtering, and fallback ownership.
