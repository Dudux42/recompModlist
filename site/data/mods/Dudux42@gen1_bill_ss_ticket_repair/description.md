# Gen1 Bill S.S. Ticket Repair

Fixes Bill's Cell Separator and S.S. Ticket progression in Pokémon Red, Blue,
and Yellow without replacing the original quest or dialogue.

- Recovers saves interrupted after the Cell Separator ran but before human
  Bill became available.
- Restores one missing S.S. Ticket when the save already records that it was
  received, provided it is not in the Bag or item PC and the S.S. Anne has not
  departed.
- Repairs the vanilla Bill fallback used by Echoes Beyond the Fog 2.2.0 while
  preserving that mod's own quest conversations.

Fresh and unstarted Bill quests remain untouched. The mod does not duplicate
tickets or automatically complete the quest.
